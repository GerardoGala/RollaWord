RollAnimal Photo Fetcher

This page finds a free-to-use photo for every animal in animal-data.csv. It runs in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and needs the internet. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Choose your animal-data.csv.
  2. Press Start. Try 5 animals first, or type a few names (such as Lynx, Bison) to fetch just those. Then clear both boxes to do them all (about 10 minutes). Keep this tab open while it works.
  3. Look at the photos. For one you do not like (or one that failed), paste a photo's file name from commons.wikimedia.org under it, such as File:Plains_Zebra.jpg (or the whole page address), and press Use. Check the photo's licence on its Commons page first.
  4. Press the three Download buttons. Unzip the photos into your rollanimal folder (so you get rollanimal/img/moth.jpg, rollanimal/img/polar-bear.jpg ...), and replace js/animal-data.csv with the new CSV. Put credits.html in the rollanimal folder too.
Choose your animal-data.csv to begin.
