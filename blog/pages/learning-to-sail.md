# Learning to Sail: A Beginner's First Steps

*Sample article · Replace this content when the publishing console is ready.*

Sailing begins with learning to observe the wind, understand the boat, and make
small, deliberate adjustments.

## Start with the wind

Before leaving shore, notice the wind direction and strength. Understanding
where the wind is coming from helps you make safer decisions on the water.

## Learn progressively

A good beginner lesson focuses on a few fundamentals at a time:

1. Boat parts and basic rigging.
2. Wind direction and points of sail.
3. Steering and sail trim.
4. Tacking and gybing.
5. Capsize recovery and safety.

Enjoy the learning process, and always sail with appropriate supervision and
safety equipment.
