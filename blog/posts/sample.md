# Exploring the San Juan Coastline

Located on the southeastern tip of Batangas, **San Juan** is famous for its long stretch of coastline facing Tayabas Bay. Whether you are looking for a quick weekend escape from the city or a quiet place to unwind by the sea, this coastal town has something for everyone.

## Why Visit the Coast?

* **Accessible Beachfront:** Most resorts sit directly along the shore, giving you instant access to soft sand and gentle waves.
* **Calm Waters:** The bay generally offers calm swimming conditions, making it great for families and casual swimmers.
* **Water Activities:** Visitors can easily rent kayaks, paddleboards, or join short boat tours to nearby coves and snorkeling spots.

## Popular Coastal Spots

### Laiya
Laiya is the most well-known stretch of beach in San Juan. It features white sand, clear blue water, and a wide variety of accommodations ranging from cozy cottages to full-service resorts.

### Hugom
Located just past the main Laiya strip, Hugom offers a quieter, more rustic atmosphere. It is a favorite starting point for hikers heading up Mt. Daguldol before cooling off in the sea.

## Quick Tips Before You Go

1. **Travel Early:** Driving down on a weekend morning helps you beat highway traffic and gives you a full day by the water.
2. **Pack Reef-Safe Sunscreen:** Help protect the local marine life while enjoying the tropical sun.
3. **Bring Cash:** While larger resorts accept cards or e-wallets, smaller local eateries and boat operators still prefer cash.
