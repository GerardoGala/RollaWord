My First Guava Fruit

Lucas Timothy November 26, 2026
Farmland
  • The sharp, savory crunch of the salt cuts right through the tartness of the skin and brings out the subtle sweetness of the fruit. It is a simple farm-to-table snack, and a great milestone for our little orchard.
  • Fresh off the branch, their crisp green skin had that unmistakable earthy, sweet aroma that store-bought fruit rarely matches. I brought them straight to the kitchen and sliced one open on the chopping board to see how they turned out inside.
  • There is something uniquely rewarding about harvesting the very first fruit from a tree you have been tending on the farm. After weeks of watching the blossoms turn into small green buds and slowly swell under the sun, I finally picked my first batch of fresh guavas.If you ever grow your own guava tree, give it plenty of direct sunlight, prune back leggy branches after fruiting to encourage denser blooms, and harvest the fruit while the skin is still firm and bright green with just a hint of yellow so you get that unbeatable crisp crunch before birds or fruit flies beat you to it.
