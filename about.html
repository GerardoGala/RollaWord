About RollaWord

RollaWord is a free, Scrabble-style word game you can play in your browser, on your phone or on your computer. No download and no sign-up.

Play now

How it works

Each round gives you a rack of eight colored letter tiles and a three-minute timer. Arrange tiles into words, score points for the letters you use, and try to beat your best. Every rack is built so that it can always be played, so you are never stuck with an impossible set of letters.

  • Free Play: a fresh rack every time, for as long as you like.
  • Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same rack for the day. Share the link with friends and compare scores on that day's leaderboard.
  • Use all eight tiles for a big bonus, and the faster you do it, the better the bonus.
  • See the solution: after a game you can look at the best words for the rack and tap a word to read its definition.

Why we made it

RollaWord started as a way to enjoy a quick daily word puzzle with friends and family, without long sessions or complicated rules. It is made and maintained by an independent developer, and we keep improving it based on player feedback.

Word list

The game uses a built-in list of common English words so that every player is judged by the same dictionary. If you think a valid word is missing, let us know on the Contact page.
