About Us

RollaWord is a free, Scrabble-style word game you can play in your browser, on your phone or on your computer. No download and no sign-up.

Play now

How it works

Each round gives you a rack of eight colored letter tiles and a three-minute timer. Arrange tiles into words, score points for the letters you use, and try to beat your best. Every rack is built so that it can always be played, so you are never stuck with an impossible set of letters.

  • Free Play: a fresh rack every time, for as long as you like.
  • Daily Challenge: everyone gets the same rack for the day. Share the link with friends and compare scores on that day's leaderboard.
  • Use all eight tiles for a big bonus, and the faster you do it, the better the bonus.
  • See the solution: after a game you can look at the best words for the rack and tap a word to read its definition.

Why we made it

RollaWord started as a way to enjoy a quick daily word puzzle with friends and family, without long sessions or complicated rules. It is made and maintained by an independent developer, and we keep improving it based on player feedback.

Word list

The game uses a built-in list of common English words so that every player is judged by the same dictionary. If you think a valid word is missing, let us know on the Contact page.

About Thesaurus

Thesaurus is a free, tap-to-explore word game that lives alongside RollaWord. Type a word and watch its synonyms appear around it, then keep tapping to wander from word to word. No download and no sign-up.

Play now

How it works

Type any word into the box at the top and press Enter. Up to nine one-word synonyms pop out of the box and settle into a grid underneath it. Tap any of them and it becomes the new center word, with a fresh set of synonyms of its own. See how far you can travel from where you started.

  • Tap to explore: every synonym is a button, so each tap takes you one step further along the trail.
  • Prev and Next: every word you visit is remembered in order. Use Prev to step back along your trail, and Next to move forward again.
  • One-word answers: multi-word phrases are left out, so what you see is always a single word.

Why we made it

Thesaurus began as a companion to RollaWord, a relaxed way to play with words and pick up a few new ones when you want a break from the timer. Like RollaWord, it is designed for phones first and is made by the same independent developer.

Where the words come from

Synonyms are looked up through the free Datamuse word-finding service. When you search, the word you type is sent to Datamuse to fetch its synonyms. If a word you expect is missing, or something looks wrong, let us know on the Contact page.

Get in touch

Found a bug or have an idea for a new feature, in either game? We would like to hear from you. See our Contact page, and read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.
