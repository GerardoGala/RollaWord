RollaCity Game Guide

Welcome to RollaCity! Your objective is to form intersecting words on the matrix using your mixed tray of letters before the countdown timer hits zero. Every word is the name of a country or a capital city, so keep your eyes open for words like PERU, OSLO or CANBERRA.

  • Select FREE PLAY to roll a randomized hand, or choose DAILY CHALLENGE to play a globally synchronized daily board.
  • Drag and drop your character tiles directly from the bottom row tray up onto the 6x8 grid board cells.
  • Rearrange your letters at any point during active countdown play.
  • When you have linked all your paths successfully together, hit the Done button to freeze the layout and submit your entries for engine calculation.

Points are computed based on standard Scrabble metrics, along with strict layout validation rules:

  • Each single letter has an integrated point value shown on the lower-right corner of the tile face. Blank tiles (*) carry zero base points but automatically optimize into any target letter that yields the highest cumulative scoring hand.
  • Color Multipliers: Form a complete valid word using tiles entirely of one matching color to trigger a bonus score overlay! Blue lines apply a x2 multiplier, while Red lines apply a x3 multiplier. These multipliers are clearly added to the individual word calculations.
  • The Word List: Only the names of the countries that are members of the United Nations, and their capital cities, count. Every name is cut to its first 8 letters with spaces and punctuation removed, because the board is 8 columns wide. So Washington becomes WASHINGT, the Philippines become PHILIPPI, and New Zealand becomes NEWZEALA. Names of 8 letters or fewer stay as they are, such as PERU, OSLO and CANBERRA. The complete list, with exactly what to type for each name, is in the Word List section below.
  • The Moat Rule: All tiles placed onto the playing matrix must form a single, interconnected island block. Separate standalone letter groups are invalid.
  • Invalid Contamination Cascading: Ensure every single word on the board is a country or capital on the word list! If an invalid word is discovered, a black line is drawn across it and it scores 0 points. Furthermore, any valid word that intersects or shares a letter tile with that invalid word becomes contaminated and is instantly disqualified.
  • 8-Tile Word Bonus: Just like the real Scrabble rule, if you use all 8 tiles in one single valid word, you earn a flat +50 point bonus. It is added to your subtotal before the time multiplier is applied, and it is not multiplied by color bonuses.
  • Subtotal Output: The engine instantly sums up the points scored across all valid, non-contaminated words (plus the 8-tile word bonus, if you earned it) to display a raw SUBTOTAL value before computing final jackpot bonuses.
  • 8-Dice Jackpot Bonus: Get all 8 tray dice onto the board inside completely valid, non-contaminated structures to trigger the ultimate scoring bonus:
    • If the remaining seconds left to play is greater than 1, your subtotal score gets multiplied by the exact number of remaining seconds left on the clock! This includes the 50-point bonus if you earned it.

These are all the words that count in RollaCity: every member state of the United Nations and its capital city. The Type column shows each name the way the game spells it, cut to 8 letters with no spaces or punctuation. A few countries have more than one capital, and all of them count.
