Frequently Asked Questions

What is San Juan Coast Line?

San Juan Coast Line is a small, locally run sailing and paddling operation on the beach at Laiya, San Juan, Batangas, Philippines. We teach sailing lessons, rent small sailboats to people who already sail, and rent kayaks, stand-up paddleboards and a paddling canoe to anyone who wants to explore the coast. Our sailing school is also listed on Google Maps as San Juan Academy.

Where are you located?

We are at Laiya, San Juan, Batangas. You can find our location on the map on the About Us page.

What languages do you teach in?

Sailing lessons are taught in English.

Sailing Lessons

I have never sailed. Can I still take a lesson?

Yes. Our beginner lessons are designed for people who have never been on a sailboat. A half-day lesson on an ILCA dinghy covers the basics: how a boat uses the wind, how to steer and trim the sail, how to turn the boat, and what to do if it capsizes. Personal safety comes first in every lesson.

How long is a lesson?

Lessons are half-day sessions. When you book, we will tell you the start time and how long to plan for.

Who will teach me?

Divine Orciga teaches beginner lessons, and Gerardo Gala teaches intermediate dinghy sailing with an emphasis on boat handling and racing. You can read both bios on the Instructors page.

Do I need to know how to swim?

You do not need to be an expert swimmer, but you should be comfortable in the water. Life jackets are worn on the water, and capsizing is a normal part of learning to sail.

Can children take lessons?

Please call or email us with the child's age and swimming ability, and we will tell you whether a lesson is a good fit and how to arrange it.

Rentals

What can I rent?

For sailors, we rent the ILCA 7 (₱6,900 per half-day session), the Open Skiff (₱2,500 per session) and the Kombi sailing canoe (₱4,900 per half-day session). For paddlers, we rent the Kombi paddling canoe (₱4,900 per half-day session), the XTR17 kayak (₱4,500 per session), the Tahe Beach 185 (₱3,900 per session) and Starboard Waterline Tech Touring stand-up paddleboards (₱3,900 per session). Details are on the sailboat rentals and kayak and SUP rentals pages.

Do I need experience to rent a sailboat?

Yes. Our sailboats are for people who can already sail. If you are new, take a lesson first and rent a boat afterwards. When you book, we will ask about your experience so we can recommend the right boat.

Do I need experience to rent a kayak or paddleboard?

No. Kayaks, paddleboards and the canoe are beginner friendly in calm conditions close to the beach. We will explain the basics and where to stay before you launch.

Which boat should I choose?

The ILCA 7 is the most demanding and best for experienced sailors in moderate to strong wind. The Open Skiff is a good boat for practising handling and quick sails. The Kombi sailing canoe is very light and works best at medium to high tide. If you are not sure, call us and we will help you choose.

Wind, Tide and Safety

What is the best time of day to go out?

It depends on the wind and tide. Light wind is better for first lessons and paddling, while stronger wind suits experienced sailors. Check our wind prediction for an hourly forecast in knots and our tide schedule for water depth, or ask us when you book.

Does the tide matter?

Yes. The water gets shallow farther out at low tide, which affects launching and some boats. The Kombi sailing canoe works best at medium to high tide.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We watch the weather and may change or postpone a session if conditions are not safe. We will talk to you about the options before your session.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear or quick-dry clothes, shoes that can get wet, a hat, sunscreen, drinking water and a change of clothes. A rash guard is useful if you burn easily. A waterproof pouch for your phone is a good idea.

Is it safe?

Time on the water always carries some risk. We reduce it by watching the weather, briefing every guest before launching, matching you to the right boat or lesson, and asking you to wear a life jacket and stay in the agreed area. Please tell us honestly about your experience, and come in early if you feel tired or conditions change.
