Balancing Paradise and Progress

Gerardo Gala November 16, 2026
Balancing Paradise and Progress: What’s Happening Along the San Juan, Batangas Coastline
Good day, coastal neighbors and travelers! If you’ve been keeping an eye on our beloved shores in San Juan, Batangas lately, you know that our coastline is buzzing with activity. From grassroots environmental clean-ups to major luxury resort developments, 2026 has proven to be a pivotal year for balancing nature preservation with booming tourism.
 
 
Here is a roundup of the latest news shaping the San Juan coastline right now.
Communities Rally to Keep Our Shores Clean
San Juan’s 16 coastal barangays are the lifeblood of our local economy and natural beauty, and recent community efforts show just how committed locals and agencies are to protecting them.
In late October 2026, the Batangas Provincial Statistical Office stepped away from their desks and headed to the shores of Barangay Nagsaulay. As reported by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Region IV-A, the team led a major coastal clean-up drive to mark the 37th National Statistics Month. The initiative highlighted a crucial message: keeping our coastal resources healthy is essential for sustaining local tourism.
This followed closely on the heels of the International Coastal Clean-Up in September 2026, where local schools and community volunteers joined forces along the Lawaye River in Barangay Sico 2.0. Because the river feeds directly into our coastal waters, keeping these waterways clear of debris is a massive win for our beaches and marine life.
Major Beachfront Developments Take Shape
While locals work hard to keep our waters pristine, real estate and hospitality developers are continuing to invest heavily in San Juan’s status as a premier getaway just south of Metro Manila.
In mid-2026, PhilSTAR Property spotlighted the ongoing progress of Solmera Coast, DMCI Homes Leisure Residences' massive beach park condotel and events destination rising right along our shores. With construction passing the 37% completion mark this year, the project is set to bring a fresh wave of tropical-inspired leisure spaces and event venues to the area.
Meanwhile, Laiya continues to level up its luxury offerings. Earlier this year, BusinessMirror took a closer look at Laiya’s evolving coastal charm, featuring Landco Lifestyle Ventures' hospitality footprint alongside The Spinnaker—a striking 22-story beachfront condominium overlooking Tayabas Bay.
Looking Ahead
From the quiet, community-driven clean-up efforts in Nagsaulay and Sico 2.0 to the towering new developments in Laiya, the story of the San Juan coastline in 2026 is one of dual momentum. As more visitors and homeowners discover the magic of our beaches, these ongoing conservation drives remind us that protecting our natural shoreline remains everyone’s top priority.
Stay tuned to the San Juan Coast Line Blog for more local updates, travel tips, and community stories from around town!
