4. HOW THE NOVEMBER 21 FINAL WARNING COMPARES WITH SEC. 9
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - It is labeled a warning, not a Sec. 9(b) notice. It gives no 15-day
   period to explain and sets no hearing.
 - It threatens Non-Member in Good Standing status and suspension. Sec. 9
   and 10 require notice, an opportunity to explain, a hearing, and a
   majority Board vote first.
 - It is signed by Rolando Conejos Jr., one of the directors whose recusal
   my counsel requested in April.
 - It does not cite the specific rule or Board resolution that makes the
   Beach Park locations non-designated.
 - It treats the July 25 First Written Warning as a prior warning, although
   that letter said no final determination had been made.
 - Its own Section V says sanctions would follow only after "proper
   investigation and observance of due process" for a subsequent incident.
   It sets no deadline for me to reply and no hearing date.
